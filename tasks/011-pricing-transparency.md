# PRICING TRANSPARENCY

Goal:

Explain how estimates are composed to build user trust.

Activities:

* add pricing transparency block to landing page:
  * show that estimates include: materials + labor + preparation + regional adjustment
  * show confidence range: ±10–15%
  * show 3–4 example line items (e.g. "Wall painting Warsaw: 25–40 PLN/m²")
* write methodology section (accessible from landing or /methodology page):
  * explain estimation formula in plain language
  * explain what regional multipliers are and why they differ by city
  * explain labor rate assumptions (rates sourced from Polish market, 2024)
  * explain material assumptions (Economy / Standard / Premium tiers defined)
  * explain supporting materials overhead (10–15% of materials)
  * explain what is NOT included (contractor fees, permits, design)
* add confidence range indicator to estimate output:
  * show ±10–15% label near the total estimate
  * show "Based on [city] regional pricing" below the estimate total
* add trust indicators:
  * "No account needed"
  * "Based on real Polish market data"
  * "Estimate in under 10 minutes"
  * "±10–15% precision"

Output:

landing pricing block, methodology page or section, confidence range indicator in estimate

Validation:

user can understand where numbers come from before and after running an estimate
